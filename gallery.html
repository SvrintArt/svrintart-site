Dimensions: 100 x 60 cm   |   Technique: Acrylic and sand on canvas (Conceptual material painting)   |   Year: 2026   |   Status: Available

CONCEPT:

The cosmos has its laws, which science can only partially explain. Near a black hole, tidal forces caused by differences in gravity tend to stretch and tear bodies apart; time slows down, visual light trajectories are deflected, and light itself is swallowed. These mysterious and fascinating entities, born from the implosione of old stars, draw everything beyond the event horizon—a place where the unknown reigns.

It is my hope that the black holes of our subconscious can also swallow everything we cannot let go of, causing it to vanish beyond the event horizon of the dark side of our minds—a place we all possess, know we possess, but carefully avoid visiting.

SGUARDO AMEBICO DEL GENITORE

CONCEPT:

The profound parental crisis of the third millennium, both educational and identity-based, erases the defined features of the human face, replacing them with a fluid, unstable, and primitive form: an amoeba. The "amoebic gaze" mourns the emotional bewilderment and the lack of that pedagogical firmness necessary to guide new generations, exposing the dramatic consequences of this relational void. Deprived of boundaries and reference points, unused to renunciation and defeat, children develop deep existential disorders. Contemporary violence and anger do not come from nowhere; they are the painful outlet of this loneliness and disorientation.

Through the contrasts of matter and color, the canvas aims to be an uncomfortable mirror of our time: a visceral call to parental responsibility and presence.
